Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

Bibelappen is designed to respect your privacy. Your Bible reading data syncs via your personal iCloud account — we never see it. We do not collect personal information, we do not track you across apps, and we do not sell data to third parties.

Data stored on your device and in iCloud

Bibelappen stores the following data locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your reading progress, bookmarks, and highlights
  • Journal entries and personal reflections
  • Onboarding preferences and life stage selection
  • Level, XP, streaks, and achievements
  • Reading plan enrollments and challenge progress

If you are signed into iCloud, this data syncs automatically between your devices via Apple's CloudKit. The data is stored in your personal iCloud account — Wideout AB does not have access to it. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to this sync.

Bible text and cached content (devotionals, study questions, verse context) are stored locally only and do not sync to iCloud.

AI-powered features

Bibelappen uses AI for the daily insight and devotional, verse context, study questions, the daily quiz, free-text Bible questions, and AI-generated reading plans and collections. When you use these features, your request is sent to our proxy server — a Cloudflare Worker — which forwards it to OpenAI. We do not store your requests or the responses.

What the request contains depends on the feature: a Bible reference, the text of a question you typed, a topic you described, or — for the daily insight and devotional — the book and chapter references of your last few readings, so the text can respond to what you have been reading. It never contains your name, email, Apple ID, or any identifier that ties a request to you personally, and OpenAI never sees your IP address, because the call is made by our server rather than by your device.

OpenAI retains API requests for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring before deleting them. That is OpenAI's default policy, not something we control, and it is why anything you type into an AI feature should be treated as leaving your device. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

To keep the service working and its costs bounded, our proxy stores two things: a per-device key registered through Apple's App Attest (a key identifier and a public key, kept for up to 400 days, which proves requests come from a genuine copy of the app), and short-lived request counters. Where no device key is available, a counter may be keyed to your IP address for up to 48 hours. Neither holds anything you wrote or read.

Analytics

Bibelappen uses TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used in aggregate (e.g., which features are popular, where users drop off). TelemetryDeck is privacy-focused by design:

  • No personal identifiers are sent. Anonymous hashed identifiers rotate periodically.
  • No IP address logging, no cookies, no cross-app tracking.
  • Analytics data is aggregated and used only to improve Bibelappen.

See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy for details.

Crash reporting

Bibelappen uses Sentry to capture crash reports and fix bugs. Crash reports include device type, iOS version, and a stack trace. They do not include personally identifiable information. Bible verse text, journal content, AI prompts, and AI responses are explicitly stripped from crash reports before they are sent.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Bibelappen Premium, payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, which stores an anonymous identifier tied to your Apple device. We do not receive or store your payment information.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your contacts, photos, location, or microphone
  • The content of your journal entries (stored locally and in your iCloud only)
  • Your reading history on our servers — it stays on your device and in your iCloud. The book and chapter references of your last few readings are sent to OpenAI when you use the daily insight or devotional, as described above, but we do not keep them.

Who processes data for us, and where

Wideout AB is the data controller. We use these processors, and no others:

  • Cloudflare — hosts the AI proxy and the App Attest key and rate-limit records described above.
  • OpenAI — generates the AI text.
  • TelemetryDeck — anonymous usage analytics.
  • Sentry — crash reports.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status.
  • Apple — the App Store, and your own iCloud, which we cannot read.

Several of these are based outside the EU/EEA, so data processed by them may be transferred to and processed in the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and on Standard Contractual Clauses otherwise.

Your rights

Under GDPR and other applicable laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because Bibelappen stores your data locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account, you can delete it at any time by removing your data in the app or by uninstalling it. For questions about data we may hold via TelemetryDeck, Sentry, RevenueCat, Cloudflare or OpenAI, contact us below.

Children

Bibelappen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we update this policy, the revision date above will change. Material changes will be announced in the app.
