This is the most important term on this page. Redo helps you prepare before something happens. It does not summon help, does not monitor you, and does not alert you to danger. It cannot function if your phone is off, out of battery, damaged, or without a connection.

In an emergency, call 112. During a crisis, follow the instructions of emergency services, listen to Sveriges Radio P4, and consult Krisinformation.se. Never delay acting on official instructions in order to consult this app, and never let anything shown here override what emergency services tell you.

What the app is

Redo is an informational tool that helps a household record what supplies it holds, see roughly how long those supplies would last, locate public shelters from the official register, and read crisis information published by Krisinformation.se.

It is general guidance for a general household. It is not professional advice, and it does not account for your particular medical needs, dependants, dwelling, or circumstances. You remain responsible for your own preparedness decisions.

Accuracy of information

Redo draws on public sources: the shelter register published by Myndigheten för civilt försvar, and crisis information from Krisinformation.se. Preparedness recommendations follow MCF's published advice for households.

We take accuracy seriously, but we cannot guarantee it. Source data may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect; shelters may be full, locked, restricted, or unusable; a copy stored on your device may be older than what has since been published; and network problems may prevent updates from arriving. Readiness figures are estimates produced from what you told the app, and they are only as accurate as what you entered.

Always verify against official sources before acting on anything shown in Redo. Where the app and an official source disagree, the official source is correct. If you spot an error, please tell us at info@wideout.se.
