Support & Help

Last updated: August 2026

In an emergency, call 112

Redo is a preparedness tool, not an emergency service. It does not call for help, does not alert you, and cannot reach you if your phone is off or without a connection. In an emergency, call 112. For official information during a crisis, listen to Sveriges Radio P4 and follow Krisinformation.se.

Get in touch

Email us at info@wideout.se. We respond within two business days. When reporting a problem, please include your iOS version, device model, and a short description of what happened — Redo's Inställningar → Rapportera problem fills those in for you.

Frequently asked questions

A quantity in the app looks wrong. What do I do?

Tell us. Redo's recommendations are based on official guidance from Myndigheten för civilt försvar, and if a figure disagrees with the source, that is a problem we want to fix quickly. Email info@wideout.se with the item and what you believe the correct figure is. Corrections to preparedness guidance are the feedback we most want to receive.

The authoritative source is always MCF and Krisinformation.se, not Redo.

Why does my readiness show 0 days when I have added things?

The day count is limited by whichever category you have least of. Two weeks of food and one day of water is one day of readiness, because you would run out of water first. That is deliberate — an average would tell you that you are prepared when you are not.

The thin progress bar underneath moves as soon as you add anything, so your effort is visible even while the headline number is still honestly 0.

Why does the app want my location?

Only to show where you are in relation to the nearest skyddsrum. It is requested when you open the map, never at launch, and your position is held in memory only — never stored, never sent anywhere. You can decline and still use the map by searching.

Does Redo work without a connection?

Yes. The shelter dataset and all guidance are stored in the app, so the map and the checklists work with no connection at all. Only VMA and crisis news need the internet, and Redo will show you the last information it retrieved along with when it retrieved it, rather than pretending to be current.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not currently. Redo stores everything on the device and syncs nothing, which is what allows it to collect nothing about you. The trade-off is that a new phone starts empty. If restoring from an iPhone backup does not bring your data across, you will need to add your supplies again.

How do I stop the reminders?

Open Inställningar in Redo and turn off rotation reminders, or turn off notifications for Redo entirely in iOS Settings. Redo sends at most one reminder a month, never at night, and never when there is nothing to tell you.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Everything Redo knows is stored on your device, so removing the app removes all of it. There is no server-side copy and no account to close.

Is the shelter information guaranteed to be correct?

No. Redo shows the official register as published, and that register can be incomplete or out of date. Some shelters are marked as temporarily restricted. Always follow the instructions of emergency services and official channels over anything shown in the app.

Does Redo cost anything?

No. Redo is free, contains no advertising, and has no in-app purchases.

Data sources

Shelter data comes from Myndigheten för civilt försvar's public register and is used as open data. Crisis alerts and VMA come from Krisinformation.se. Preparedness guidance follows MCF's published advice for households.

Requirements

Redo requires iOS 18 or later on iPhone.
