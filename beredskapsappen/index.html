Redo · iOS

Hemberedskap, utan krångel.

Redo helps Swedish households get genuinely prepared. Track what you have at home and in the car, find your nearest skyddsrum, and follow official VMA alerts — in one calm, Swedish-language app.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What Redo does

Know how many days you are covered

Add what you actually have — water, food, warmth, light — and Redo works out how long it would last your household. One honest number, and a clear next step to improve it.

Every skyddsrum in Sweden

All 63,000+ public shelters, mapped from the official register, with capacity and address. Works offline — the map you need in a crisis should not depend on a working connection.

Official alerts, nothing invented

VMA and crisis information straight from Krisinformation.se, clearly attributed. Redo never writes its own emergency messages.

Supplies that stay fresh

A krislåda quietly expires. Redo tracks best-before dates and reminds you to eat and replace — at most once a month, never at night, and never asking you to throw food away.

The car counts too

A separate winter and summer checklist for the car, including what Swedish law actually requires. Being stuck on the E22 is the emergency most of us are most likely to meet.

Collects nothing at all

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no servers holding your information. What you own and where you live stays on your phone. This is a deliberate design decision, not a setting.

In an emergency, call 112

Redo is a preparedness tool, not an emergency service and not an alerting system. It cannot reach you if your phone is off, out of battery, or without a connection. In an emergency, call 112. For official information during a crisis, listen to Sveriges Radio P4 and follow Krisinformation.se.
