Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Summary

Redo collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours that holds anything about you. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This is a deliberate design decision rather than a setting you have to find. An app that records what a household owns and where it lives is holding information worth protecting, and the strongest way to protect it is not to collect it.

What stays on your device

Redo stores the following locally, using Apple's SwiftData framework, inside the app's own private container:

  • Your household composition (number of adults, children, and pets)
  • The supplies you have recorded, their quantities and best-before dates
  • Whether you have added a vehicle, and what is in it
  • Your settings, including reminder preferences

This data is not synced to iCloud and not transmitted anywhere. It exists on the one device you entered it on. If you delete the app, it is gone — we hold no copy and cannot recover it for you.

The home-screen widget reads a small summary — your day count and one suggested next step — from a shared container on the same device. That summary never leaves the device either.

Location

If you use the shelter map, Redo asks for location access so it can show where you are in relation to the nearest skyddsrum. Permission is requested only when you open the map, never at launch.

Your position is held in memory for as long as the map is open and is then discarded. It is never written to storage, never saved to the database, never included in any file, never logged, and never transmitted. Redo does not use background location and cannot track your movements. You can decline, and the map still works — you simply search instead.

Network requests

Redo contacts two external sources, and only to fetch information:

  • Krisinformation.se — for VMA and crisis news
  • The official shelter register (Myndigheten för civilt försvar, via its public map service) — for updates to the skyddsrum dataset

These requests carry no identifier, no account, no device ID, and nothing derived from anything you entered. They are ordinary downloads: Redo asks for public information and receives it. Nothing about you is sent.

As with any request to any website, the servers you contact necessarily see your device's IP address in order to reply. That is a property of how the internet works rather than something Redo transmits, and those services' own privacy practices apply. Wideout AB never receives, sees, or logs your IP address.

Crash reports

Redo contains no crash-reporting or analytics software. We use no third-party SDKs of any kind.

If a crash report reaches us, it is because you chose to share one with developers in iOS itself, under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple collects and anonymises those reports; we see a stack trace, an iOS version and a device model. They contain nothing you entered into Redo. You can turn that off in iOS at any time, and Redo will neither know nor behave differently.

If you email us

Redo can open a pre-filled message to our support address. You see the whole message before anything is sent, you can edit or delete any part of it, and you can cancel. It includes only the app version, your iOS version and your device model — never your supplies, your household, or your location.

If you do write to us, we keep your email only as long as it takes to answer you.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or any account — there is no sign-up
  • Your location, in any stored or transmitted form
  • What supplies you own or how prepared you are
  • Usage data, feature analytics, session counts, or crash data of our own
  • Advertising identifiers — Redo shows no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or erase personal data held about you. Because Wideout AB holds no personal data from Redo, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase — a request would return nothing, not because we refuse but because nothing exists.

Your own data lives on your device and is under your control: edit or delete any entry inside the app, or remove the app to erase all of it at once. If you would like to confirm any of this, write to us.

Children

Redo is a household preparedness tool intended for adults. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we update this policy, the revision date above will change. Redo collecting nothing is a design commitment rather than a current convenience — if that were ever to change, it would be announced in the app before it took effect, not quietly amended here.
