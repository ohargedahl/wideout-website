Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

The short version

Pausen collects no personal data at all. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It contains no advertising, no third-party software of any kind, and no code that makes a network request. Nothing about you, your child or your device is transmitted anywhere, because the app has no way to transmit anything.

This policy is short because there is genuinely very little to describe. We have deliberately not padded it with clauses about data we do not have.

What is stored on your device

Pausen saves four small values on the device itself, using Apple's standard app-settings storage. They never leave the device and are not readable by any other app:

  • The session length you last chose (3, 5, 8, 12 or 20 minutes)
  • Whether bedtime dimming is switched on
  • Whether sound is switched on
  • A single number, between zero and a small maximum, recording roughly how much of the on-screen behaviour a child has already met, so a session can resume near where the last one finished

That last number is the whole of the app's memory of a child. It is not a profile: there are no dates, no counts and no history, so it cannot be used to reconstruct when or how often the app was used, and it is never combined with anything else. Pausen never asks for a child's name, age, date of birth, photograph or any other detail — there is no screen on which such a thing could be entered.

Deleting the app deletes all four values.

Crash reports

If Pausen crashes, iOS may hand the app a technical crash report — the kind of thing that names the line of code that failed. Pausen keeps a small number of these on the device so they can be looked at on the "Crash reports" screen, and discards the older ones.

These reports are never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no crash-reporting service in the app and no upload of any kind. Separately, and entirely under Apple's control rather than ours, iOS itself may share diagnostics with Apple if you have opted into that in Settings; that is a setting on your device, not a feature of this app, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Pausen is intended to be used by a very young child with a parent nearby. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and therefore does not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 — or under any age. There is nothing to disclose, sell or share, and no request to a parent for consent is required because no collection takes place.

Pausen contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no links out to other apps or websites, and no way for a child to contact anyone or be contacted.

Tracking

Pausen does not track you. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not fingerprint the device, and shares nothing with data brokers or advertising networks. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and that is a description of the code rather than an aspiration.

Your rights under the GDPR

Wideout AB (Stockholm, Sweden) is the party responsible for this app. Because Pausen processes no personal data on our behalf and sends us nothing, we hold no personal data about you or your child — so there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct, export or erase. The values described above are held solely on your own device and are under your control: removing the app removes them.

You are welcome to contact us with any question about this, and you have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. If a future version of Pausen ever collected anything, that would be a material change and this page would say so plainly before it shipped.
